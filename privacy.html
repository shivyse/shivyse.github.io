What the application accesses

With the operator’s Google authorization, Exclusive Intruder Automation accesses the authorized YouTube channel’s identifier and basic channel information, and video metadata, privacy and processing status needed to verify its uploads. It sends the operator-selected video files, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to YouTube.

Purpose and storage

The application uses these permissions to verify the intended channel, upload videos privately, set thumbnails and check processing. OAuth access and refresh tokens and resumable upload-session information are encrypted for the operator’s Windows account and stored locally. Production files, checkpoints and diagnostic records are stored on the operator’s device. Credentials are not included in this public site.

Sharing and AI services

Account authorization and uploads are processed by Google. Configured narration or review providers may receive story inputs explicitly selected for those production stages. The uploader does not send its OAuth credentials or YouTube account records to those models. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models by this application.

Exclusive Intruder Automation’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Credentials remain on the operator’s device until the operator disconnects the application or deletes its local encrypted credential. Job artifacts and logs remain until the operator removes them. Removing local files does not delete uploaded YouTube videos; those are managed separately in YouTube Studio.

Control and revocation

The operator may revoke access at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API work. Google and the static hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information under their own privacy policies. This information site has no analytics scripts, cookies set by the application, login form or upload form.

Questions

Contact the operator through the public Exclusive Intruder channel information. This page is updated when the tool’s data practices change.
