Exclusive Intruder Automation helps its operator prepare Hindi story videos and deliver reviewed videos to the Exclusive Intruder YouTube channel.

The application runs on the operator’s Windows computer. It keeps production checkpoints, prepares narration and visuals, applies channel branding, and uses the YouTube Data API for authorized channel checks, private video uploads, thumbnails and processing status.

One channel. Explicit access.

Google authorization is requested for YouTube video upload and read-only account access. The application verifies the intended channel before storing its connection. This site does not provide a login or collect Google credentials.

The current background uploader creates private videos. This tool does not automatically publish them publicly.
